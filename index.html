Common scams

Fake Bank Alert SMS

High

Scammers send urgent texts about account issues to steal your login details and money.

Wrong Number WhatsApp Scam

Medium

Scammers use random numbers to start conversations and gain your trust, then ask for money or personal information.

Fake Delivery Notice

Medium

You receive a fake message about a missed delivery, with a link to pay a fee or update your details.
